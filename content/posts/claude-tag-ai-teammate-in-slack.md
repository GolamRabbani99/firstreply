<p>Imagine the most reliable colleague you've ever had. You tag them into a conversation, they read the whole thread so you don't have to explain anything twice, they go away and do the work — and they message you when it's done. Now imagine they never sleep and never get bored of being tagged.</p>

<p>That's the pitch behind <strong>Claude Tag</strong>, shown in <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VojDzHaciKQ" rel="noopener">a two-minute video on Anthropic's Claude channel</a>. It's an AI you @-mention inside Slack, exactly like a teammate.</p>

<h2>The three things that make it different from a chatbot</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>It has the channel's context.</strong> You don't brief it from scratch. Tag it into a thread and it picks up what the conversation already covered — the client's name, the deadline that moved, the decision from yesterday.</li>
<li><strong>It can use your organisation's tools.</strong> It isn't limited to writing text; it connects to the systems your team already works with.</li>
<li><strong>It persists.</strong> This is the quiet headline: it can see work through over days, and comes back to you when it's finished or needs your input. It behaves like a colleague with a to-do list, not a vending machine.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this matters even if you don't use Slack</h2>

<p>Most small businesses I work with don't run on Slack — they run on WhatsApp, email and shouting across the kitchen. But the pattern matters more than the product: <strong>AI is moving into the places where work already happens</strong>, instead of asking you to come to it.</p>

<p>We've been building on this exact principle for a while. Our speed-to-lead systems put the AI inside WhatsApp — a new enquiry arrives, the AI drafts a reply, and the owner gets a WhatsApp message with an approve button. Same idea, different room: the AI comes to you, holds the context, and a human makes the final call.</p>

<h2>What to take from it</h2>

<p>If you run a team, ask one question: <em>"Which conversations in my business repeat the same work every week?"</em> Enquiry triage, meeting prep, chasing missing information, drafting the same three documents. Those threads are where an AI teammate — whichever tool it lives in — earns its keep first.</p>
