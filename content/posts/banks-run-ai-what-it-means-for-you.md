<p>Banks are not early adopters. They are the industry of triple sign-offs, compliance departments bigger than most companies, and regulators who can end careers with a letter. When banks move on a technology, it isn't a trend any more — it's infrastructure.</p>

<p>So it's worth pausing on a quiet announcement from <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H3XQeGNia8o" rel="noopener">Anthropic's Claude channel</a>: <strong>some of the world's largest banks are now running Claude across their organisations</strong> — and the leaders behind that work presented what they've built at an event called The Briefing: Financial Services.</p>

<h2>Why this one matters more than the flashy demos</h2>

<p>AI playing games or generating videos makes better viewing. But a bank deploying AI means something much more boring and much more important: the technology passed <strong>security review, compliance review, data-protection review and risk review</strong> — the four horsemen that kill most software projects in regulated industries.</p>

<p>For every business owner who's been told (or told themselves) "AI isn't safe enough for customer data yet", this is the recalibration point. The most audited industry on earth looked at the risks, engineered around them, and shipped.</p>

<h2>The catch: they didn't just switch it on</h2>

<p>Banks didn't paste customer accounts into a free chatbot. They deployed AI <em>with controls</em>: defined data boundaries, audit trails, human sign-off on consequential actions. The lesson isn't "AI is safe" — it's that <strong>AI is safe the way anything is safe: by design</strong>.</p>

<p>Scaled to a small business, the same design principles look like this:</p>

<ul>
<li>Customer data stays in systems you control, with the minimum collected in the first place.</li>
<li>Every automated message passes a human approval gate before sending.</li>
<li>You can see a log of what the system did and when.</li>
</ul>

<p>That's not enterprise luxury — it's exactly how we build lead pipelines for catering companies and tutoring businesses. GDPR-by-design isn't a badge on our website because it sounds nice; it's the same discipline the banks used, at a scale you can afford.</p>
