The problem

Leads go cold in minutes. Most businesses reply in hours.

Someone fills in your form at 9pm. They hear back at 11am — after they've already booked your competitor. Research on lead response has shown the same thing for years: the odds of ever reaching an enquiry collapse within the first hour. And that's only half the leak.

The tracking hole

Since iOS 14, the Meta pixel misses a chunk of your conversions. Meta then optimises your spend on incomplete data — so you pay more for worse leads and can't prove which ads actually work.

The response gap

Enquiries land at evenings and weekends — exactly when nobody's watching the inbox. Every hour of silence is money you already spent, walking off to whoever replied first.

The follow-up fade

No reply doesn't mean no interest. Most enquiries need two or three polite nudges before they book — and almost nobody sends them consistently. So the lead just… fades.

Frequently asked questions

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. Every pipeline is built GDPR-by-design: data minimisation, consent-aware event flows, UK/EU data residency where available, and your customer data is never sold or reused for anything else. The founder holds an MSc in Computer Network & System Security, and a data processing agreement is available for every engagement.

Will AI message my customers without approval?

No — never. Every automation includes a human approval gate. The AI drafts the reply; a human (you, or us on the Growth Engine) approves it with one tap before anything sends. You also sign off every message template before the system goes live. This is a hard rule, not a setting.

How long does setup take?

The Tracking Fix typically takes about a week. The Speed-to-Lead System takes one to two weeks, including your approval of every message template. The Full Growth Engine ramps over the first thirty days. You'll see the first automated reply go out before we send our final invoice.

Do I need to change my website or buy new software?

No. We build on top of what you already have — your existing website forms, Meta lead ads, email inbox and WhatsApp. The automations run on n8n, and you keep ownership of and access to everything we build. If we ever part ways, the system stays yours.

Do you only work with businesses in London?

No. We're based in London, but everything we deliver — tracking, automations, ads management, reporting — is built and run remotely. Calls happen over video, the systems live in the cloud, and the results are the same whether your business is in Manchester, Madrid or Melbourne.

Am I tied into a long contract?

No. One-off builds have no contract at all. Monthly plans are rolling with thirty days' notice — no long contracts, ever. If the system stops paying for itself, you stop paying for the system. That's the deal.
