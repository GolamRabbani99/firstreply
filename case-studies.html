Diji Catering — London

The situation: a London catering company running paid ads, with enquiries arriving by website form and email. Enquiries peaked on evenings and weekends — exactly when the team was mid-service and physically unable to reply. Average first response: around four hours. For a Saturday-night wedding enquiry, that often meant Monday.

What we built: every enquiry source captured into one pipeline; AI drafts a reply in the owner's voice within seconds; the owner approves with one tap on WhatsApp — even between courses; automated polite follow-ups until the enquiry answers; and server-side Meta tracking so every pound of ad spend is traceable to real enquiries.

4 hours → 60 seconds

Average first-response time, before → after. Including Saturday nights.

100% human-approved

Every reply reviewed and approved by the owner before sending. No exceptions.

0 enquiries missed

Since launch: every enquiry captured and followed up on schedule.
